          Faster loading
          
      
      
          USB drives are slow to read, so big models can take a minute or more to load. With this on, each model is copied to this computer's internal disk after it first loads, and later loads read from there, typically several times faster. Only the public AI model files are copied — never your chats, documents or memory. Uses up to 40 GB of this computer's disk.
          
      
          
        
        
      
          
      
          Voice
          
      
          Press 🎤 to talk instead of typing, or 📞 for a live voice call. Your voice is turned into text by a speech model on this drive (checking…), and recordings are never saved. With 🔊 Speak replies on, answers are read aloud by natural voices from this drive (Kokoro) or this computer's own voices; each agent can have its own. None of it uses the internet.
